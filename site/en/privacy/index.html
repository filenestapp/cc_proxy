Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what aifree collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights.

What we collect

  • Account: your email, a hash of your password (never the password itself), sign-up and verification times, and language preference.
  • Subscriptions and orders: plan, subscription status, order amounts and dates, and Stripe customer and order IDs. Card details are collected and processed by Stripe; we never see your full card number.
  • Devices: the name you give each device, its operating system, when it first signed in and was last seen, and the IP address it used to reach our control service.
  • Exit usage: byte counts of traffic through your exit, for operations and abuse prevention.
  • Mailbox: if you open an @aifree.sg mailbox, your mail is stored with the cloud provider we use.
  • Support: emails you send us and our replies.

What we don't collect

  • The content of pages you visit or your conversations with AI services. Connections through the exit are mostly encrypted; we don't decrypt or store their contents.
  • Traffic to non-AI sites: it goes directly from your device and never reaches our servers.
  • This website, aifree.sg, sets no cookies and loads no analytics or advertising scripts.

How we use it

  • To run the service: sign-in, assigning your exit, syncing client settings, setting up seats and mailboxes.
  • Billing: subscriptions, renewals, refunds, receipts.
  • Security: spotting unusual sign-ins, limiting password guessing, handling abuse reports.
  • Notices: verification, password reset, and subscription or service changes. We don't send marketing email unless you opt in.

Sharing and third parties

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to provide the service with:

  • Stripe, for payments and subscriptions.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts this website, the mailbox service and outgoing email.
  • Server and network providers hosting our control service and exit nodes.
  • An environment-check service: only when you run Environment check in the client, it sends observed exit addresses, system language, time zone, OS version and timings to a third-party checking service to produce the report.

We may disclose information to authorities when the law requires it.

Retention

  • Account and order data are kept while your account exists. After you delete your account, order records are kept as long as accounting and tax rules require; everything else is deleted within 30 days.
  • Device records are deleted when you sign a device out or delete your account.
  • When a subscription ends, the mailbox is disabled and later deleted after we notify you.

Your rights

You can view and change your account details, sign out devices and cancel your subscription in the account center. To export or delete your data, email support@aifree.sg; we'll respond within 30 days.

Security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Sessions use HTTPS-only cookies. The client stores a device token, not your password. You can sign out any device from the account center at any time.

Cookies

The account center at app.aifree.sg uses one essential session cookie to keep you signed in. No tracking cookies.

Changes

When we update this policy we change the date at the top, and we'll email you about significant changes.
